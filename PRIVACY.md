# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 15, 2026**

## Overview

**DEADGRID does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your game progress is saved locally on your device only.**

## Information Collection and Use

DEADGRID is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information or data from users.

## Data Storage

All game data is stored **locally on your device only**, including:

- High scores
- Unlocked weapons  
- Purchased skins
- Game statistics
- Settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

## Analytics and Tracking

DEADGRID does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We do not track your usage, behavior, or any other metrics.

## Third-Party Services

DEADGRID does not integrate with or share data with any third-party services.

## Children's Privacy

DEADGRID does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this document with an updated revision date.
