## Features

- **The Climb** — a campaign ladder thousands of levels deep. Every level rolls its own horde, weather, rule and goal: one-hit-point runs, total blackouts, meteor storms, assassination contracts, armoury drops and boss fights. Clear a stage and fly on to the next city.
- **28 real places on Earth** — Times Square, Shibuya Crossing, the Champs-Élysées, Copacabana, Terreiro do Paço, the Taj Mahal, Chichén Itzá, the Giza Plateau, the Acropolis, the Dubai Fountain, Sydney Opera House, Brandenburg Gate and more, plus worlds of our own like The Garden, Sporewood, Rustyard and Cindermaw. Every layout placed by hand.
- **Ranked duels — The Arena** — duel real opponents, last one standing wins. Buy rage zombies to hunt your rival, grab a random upgrade every round and climb from Bronze to Challenger.
- **Duo co-op** — team up with a friend or matchmake with a stranger. Shared horde, revives and quick chat.
- **More ways to play** — endless survival with permadeath, INFECT, FREE FOR ALL, TEAM DEATHMATCH (six against six) and weekly tournaments with prizes for the top 400.
- **36 weapons** — pistols, SMGs, shotguns, rifles, snipers, launchers, energy weapons, flamethrowers and wilder things, with hundreds of finishes (GOLD, ONYX, SYNTHWAVE, NOSFERATU, VOIDLINE, RAINBOW…).
- **14 kinds of undead, bosses included** — walkers, runners, tanks, boomers, spitters, bladesmen who cut bullets out of the air, wardens with hooked chains, gravekeepers who raise the dead, bat swarms and bosses that shake the ground. Smart pathfinding routes them around your cover.
- **Turrets & deployables** — three turret types, barriers, claymores, explosive barrels and the legendary clapping monkey.
- **160 skins and 32 emotes** — every skin is a premium 3D character with its own signature emote and idle animation. Try any skin on your island before you buy it.
- **Progress that lasts** — a 101-tier Game Pass, seasons, missions, daily streaks, specs and bonuses, the Vault, 11 islands and a weapon rack to show off your guns.
- **Compete with the world** — global leaderboards, a top-1000 board, friends with live presence, duo invites and player profiles.
- **Cool, stable, smooth** — rebuilt natively to stay cool: it adapts to your phone's temperature, holds 60 fps in battle, drops to 30 fps in menus and barely uses power in the background.
- **20 languages.**

## Free to play

DEADGRID is free to download and play. It is funded by optional coin packs and, on iOS, by **one short ad after every 10 finished games** — shown at the next break (for example when you return to the menu), never in the middle of a match. There is no pay-to-win: coins buy cosmetics, boosts and conveniences.

## Support

### Frequently asked questions

**I played DEADGRID 2. Is my progress still there?**
Yes. DEADGRID 3 is an update of the same app, so on its first launch it brings your DEADGRID 2 save across automatically — skins, guns, coins, pass, rank and Climb level included. Just update the app on the same phone and open it; you do not need to reinstall or sign in. If you are on a new phone, see *signing in on another device* below. If something is missing, email us with your username.

**I bought coins but they did not arrive.**
Open **GET COINS** and tap **RESTORE PURCHASES**. The game re-checks any paid purchase that has not been delivered yet (it also retries automatically in the background). Coin packs are consumable, so coins that were already delivered are not added a second time. Still missing? Email us your username and, if you have it, the Apple receipt (order ID) from your purchase email.

**Why am I seeing ads? Can I change the ad privacy settings?**
DEADGRID is free, so on iOS it shows one short ad after every 10 finished games. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Before the first ad you may be asked whether DEADGRID can track you, and (in the EEA, UK and some other regions) for your ad consent. You can change your choices at any time:
- in the game: **SETTINGS → AD PRIVACY CHOICES** (shown when it applies to you);
- on your iPhone: **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
Declining does not remove ads, but they will not be personalised.

**My phone gets hot / the game feels slow.**
DEADGRID 3 adapts to your phone's temperature automatically, but you can help it:
- **SETTINGS → GAMING SETTINGS:** pick a lower graphics level (MEDIUM, LOW or SUPER LOW) and set **FRAME RATE** to **30 FPS** — this runs noticeably cooler and saves battery;
- close other apps, avoid playing while charging or in direct sunlight, and take off thick cases on long sessions;
- keep iOS and DEADGRID up to date.

**How do I sign in on another device, or keep my account safe?**
Your account is tied to your phone until you give it a password. Go to **SETTINGS → ACCOUNT → PROTECT THIS ACCOUNT** and set a password; you will get a one-time **recovery code** — write it down, it is shown only once. On the other device, open **SETTINGS → ACCOUNT → I ALREADY HAVE AN ACCOUNT** (or type your username at the start and choose to sign in) and enter your username and password. Forgot the password? Use the recovery code instead. Lost both? Email us from the address you want us to reply to, with your username.

**How do I delete my account?**
Email [support@deadgridgame.com](mailto:support@deadgridgame.com) with your username and ask for deletion. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md#en-10).

**Online play does not connect.**
Duo and Ranked need a stable internet connection. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, make sure both players have the latest version, and try again. If a match keeps failing, tell us via **SETTINGS → MESSAGE THE TEAM**.

## Privacy

Short version of our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) (English, Português, Deutsch, Español, Français):

- You play with a **username**. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location.
- Your progress is saved on your phone **and synced to our game server** (hosted on Supabase), so it is backed up and can be recovered. Your username, scores and profile are visible to other players.
- **Duo co-op and Ranked** send live game data between you and the other player through our server. There is no free-text or voice chat.
- **Purchases** are paid through Apple; we never see your payment details. The purchase receipt is sent to our server only to verify it and deliver your coins.
- **Ads** on iOS come from **Google AdMob**, which may collect device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), IP address and coarse location, ad interactions and diagnostics. You control this in SETTINGS → AD PRIVACY CHOICES and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Notifications** are local reminders scheduled on your phone; no push server is used.
- We do **not sell** your personal data. DEADGRID is not directed to children under 13.
- To delete your account or ask about your data, email [support@deadgridgame.com](mailto:support@deadgridgame.com).
