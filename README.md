# DEADGRID

**Minimalist Zombie Survival for iOS**

Survive endless waves of the undead in this intense arena shooter. Pure arcade action with 33 weapons, strategic turrets, and no mercy.

---

## 🎮 About

DEADGRID is a brutally addictive zombie survival game where only your reflexes and strategy matter. Every run is permadeath—beat your high score in kills, time survived, and waves conquered.

**Key Features:**
- 🔫 **33 Unique Weapons** - Pistols, SMGs, shotguns, rifles, snipers, launchers, energy weapons, and more
- 🧟 **4 Zombie Types** - Walkers, runners, tanks, and explosive boomers with intelligent AI pathfinding
- 🛡️ **Strategic Turrets** - Build defensive positions with machine guns, snipers, and mortars
- ⚡ **Dynamic Upgrades** - Stack multiplicative power-ups each wave for unique builds
- 🎨 **10 Unlockable Skins** - Customize your character with hats and styles
- 💥 **Deployables** - Place barriers and claymores for tactical advantages
- 🚀 **Optimized Performance** - Smooth 60fps with tiny download size
- 🎵 **Real-time Audio** - All sound synthesized on-the-fly, zero binary assets

**No ads. No pay-to-win. No timers. Just pure survival.**

## 🎯 How to Play

### Controls
- **Move:** Touch and drag on the left side of the screen
- **Aim & Fire:** Touch and drag on the right side of the screen  
- **Switch Weapons:** Tap weapon icons at the bottom
- **Deploy Turrets:** Tap turret buttons when you have scrap

### Gameplay Tips
- **Weapon Unlocks:** Unlock all 33 weapons through lifetime kills across all runs
- **Turret Strategy:** Turrets taunt zombies - use them to create choke points
- **Upgrades:** Choose 1 of 3 upgrades after each wave - they stack for powerful builds
- **Zombie Types:**
  - *Walkers* - Slow but numerous
  - *Runners* - Fast and aggressive  
  - *Tanks* - High health juggernauts
  - *Boomers* - Explode on death or contact

## 🛠️ Support

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I unlock new weapons?**  
A: Weapons unlock based on your lifetime kills across all runs. Keep playing!

**Q: What do turrets do?**  
A: Turrets provide automated firepower and taunt nearby zombies, helping create strategic choke points. Buy them with scrap from kills.

**Q: How do upgrades work?**  
A: After each wave, choose 1 of 3 random upgrades. These stack multiplicatively throughout your run.

**Q: Is there multiplayer?**  
A: Currently DEADGRID is single-player focused on beating your personal high scores.

### Technical Issues

**Game running slowly?**
- Ensure iOS 15.0 or later
- Close background apps
- Restart your device

**Progress not saving?**
- Don't force-quit during gameplay
- Progress saves automatically between waves
- All data stored locally on your device
